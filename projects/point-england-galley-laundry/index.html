Project overview

Practical finishes that still look good.

A galley laundry with white cabinetry and a dark 600 × 600 porcelain floor that hides everyday marks.

The client chose a dark floor so dirt and marks do not show. Grout matched to the tile keeps the floor looking clean between washes.

How the work is done

A controlled sequence, not guesswork on the wall.

Every stage is checked before the next begins — the finish is only as good as the work underneath it.

  1. 01

    Assess the substrate

    Check flatness, junctions, fixtures and wet-area readiness before the layout is committed.

  2. 02

    Set out the complete room

    Balance tile courses and control visible cuts, corners and transitions.

  3. 03

    Fix and align

    Maintain adhesive transfer, consistent joints and alignment across adjoining surfaces.

  4. 04

    Finish and inspect

    Complete grout, movement joints, silicone and a final cleaning check.
